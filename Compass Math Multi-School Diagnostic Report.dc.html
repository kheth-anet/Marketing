Understanding Your Diagnostic Data
Unlike traditional diagnostics that measure what students currently know, ANet's Diagnostic predicts how students will perform after they've have been taught the material. Each cycle predicts performance on the main learning components (building blocks of standards) within upcoming lessons. With this forward-looking view, educators can proactively adjust instruction based on what is likely to be a strength or a challenge.
