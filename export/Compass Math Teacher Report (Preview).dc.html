Understanding Your Diagnostic Data
Unlike traditional diagnostics that measure what students currently know, ANet's Diagnostic predicts how students will perform after they've have been taught the material. Each cycle predicts performance on the main learning components (building blocks of standards) within your upcoming lessons. With this forward-looking view, you can proactively adjust instruction based on what is likely to be a strength or a challenge for your class.
